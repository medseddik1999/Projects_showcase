## Prompt du portrait

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: transparent portrait cutout for a professional Data Scientist portfolio hero.
Primary request: Create a new photorealistic professional editorial portrait of the same adult man shown in identity reference images 2 and 3, preserving his recognizable facial identity, face proportions, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, beard line, and warm medium skin tone.
Input images: Image 1 is ONLY the visual style reference for sophisticated dark portfolio presentation and warm amber rim lighting; do not use its model's face or glasses as identity. Images 2 and 3 show the person whose identity must be preserved. Ignore their phone screenshot chrome, hats, phone, stickers, background, and clothes.
Subject: Adult man with dark curly voluminous hair on top and faded sides, thick dark eyebrows, brown eyes, neat full dark beard and moustache, and a small hoop earring. Face the camera with a calm, warm, very slight smile, relaxed shoulders and naturally folded arms. Wear a fitted charcoal overshirt over a plain black T-shirt.
Style/medium: Real studio editorial photography, natural pores and skin texture, realistic anatomy, tasteful and credible professional presence.
Composition/framing: Portrait aspect ratio approximately 4:5. Waist-up composition with whole hair and both elbows safely inside the frame, balanced central subject and a small margin around the silhouette.
Lighting/mood: Soft flattering key light that keeps facial features clear, subtle warm amber rim lighting on hair and shoulders, restrained dark clothing tones.
Scene/backdrop: Genuine transparent background with alpha, clean cutout edges and preserved natural hair detail, no backdrop or floor.
Constraints: Preserve the identity in images 2 and 3; no glasses, no hat, no necklace, no logos; natural physique with no exaggerated muscles.
Avoid: Cartoon or illustration, beauty filter, waxy skin, exaggerated smile, glowing rings, halo, text, UI, background objects, baked-in shadows outside the silhouette.
```

## Prompt du visuel NLP

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: project cover image for a professional Data Scientist portfolio, financial-sentiment classification API.
Primary request: Create an original conceptual editorial 3D still life suggesting language processing into structured predictions.
Scene/backdrop: Minimal dark graphite and black setting with restrained amber and cool blue lighting.
Subject: A paired set of restrained sculptural code brackets, connected floating paper or message tiles with completely blank faces and no lettering, and a few luminous connected nodes. The components form one elegant coherent central arrangement.
Style/medium: Premium minimal editorial 3D still life, sophisticated materials and considered lighting, realistic soft shadows and subtle depth, not a playful mascot.
Composition/framing: Wide 16:9 landscape composition. Balanced central subject with generous negative space and very little clutter.
Lighting/mood: Subtle warm amber edge light balanced by a small amount of cool blue illumination, visually coherent with a refined dark and amber portfolio.
Constraints: No screenshot, interface chrome, words, labels, logos, numerals, actual chart, statistic, arrows, fabricated results, mascot or faces. Completely opaque background.
```
